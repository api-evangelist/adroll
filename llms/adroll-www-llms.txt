# AdRoll

> AdRoll is a digital advertising and marketing platform by NextRoll, Inc. that helps businesses grow with AI-powered retargeting, display ads, video ads, social ads, email, and account-based marketing — managed from a single platform.

AdRoll serves direct-to-consumer and B2B brands as well as agencies, offering audience targeting, cross-channel campaign management, attribution, and analytics. The platform integrates with major ecommerce, CRM, and marketing tools including Shopify, HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, and Google Ads.
